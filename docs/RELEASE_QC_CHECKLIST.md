# Release QC checklist

Use this checklist before every child Bazi summary release.

## Bazi accuracy

- Four Pillars and Day Master come from the calculation engine, never generated prose.
- Strong/Weak wording describes how readily Day Master qualities may appear, never the child's worth or ability.
- Every strength, support area, and Bazi-linked suggestion is traceable to the reviewed Day Master knowledge profile.
- No unsupported Profile Star, Ten Gods, Top Structure, prediction, diagnosis, or certainty claim is introduced.
- A report that fails the expert Bazi and editorial rules is withheld rather than released.

## Parent concern relevance

- Preserve the parent's original words for audit and display.
- Answer the concern actually entered; do not replace it with a broader or different issue.
- Suggestions must be specific to that concern and must not repeat the weekly action.
- Link advice to the Day Master only when the reviewed guidance genuinely supports the concern.
- If the concern cannot be answered safely and specifically, omit the concern advice instead of using a generic canned reply.

## Human writing and editorial QC

- Use natural, warm English that a 12-year-old can understand on the first reading.
- Every sentence has a clear subject; pronouns agree with the selected gender.
- Observations about the child and suggestions for the parent are visually and verbally distinct.
- Use concrete, recognisable moments rather than strings of abstract qualities.
- Vary headings, examples, sentence openings, and guidance verbs without changing the verified Bazi meaning.
- Remove repeated ideas across strengths, support areas, the concern response, weekly action, and closing.
- Read every section aloud for flow, grammar, warmth, and non-AI tone.

## Experience and mobile QC

- Form errors do not clear completed fields.
- Day, month, and year controls remain readable on a narrow phone.
- Bazi, Qimen, and Feng Shui remain visible in mobile navigation.
- The Four Pillars stay in Hour–Day–Month–Year order, with the Day Master highlighted without shifting alignment.
- Mobile headings, cards, and paragraphs fit the viewport without clipping or oversized text.
- Email, feedback, consent, privacy, terms, and data-request paths remain usable.
